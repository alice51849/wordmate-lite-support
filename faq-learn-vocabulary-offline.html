Wordmate Lite · FAQ

Is there a vocabulary app that works offline, on a plane or subway, with no account?

Wordmate Lite runs fully offline: learn 5 words in about a minute in Japanese, Korean, Spanish or any of 44 languages, with no account and no sign in.

  1. Walk a short five-word trail, hear each word read aloud, and see exactly which five words you learned when you finish.
  2. Start with real situations: 14 life paths including travel, dining, shopping, business and exams, each in small units at beginner, intermediate and advanced level.
  3. Pick the voice you prefer for each language, and save the words worth keeping, grouped by topic and level.
  4. Set a daily goal of 5, 10, 12 or 20 words and track your streak, your best run and your progress in every language and topic.
  5. You can start without paying: pick one language and one beginner path, learn your first 10 words and save up to 3. A single one-time purchase unlocks all 44 languages, all 14 paths and every level. It is not a subscription.

Everything runs offline. No account, no personal data collected, no third party advertising, no tracking. What you learn stays on your device.
