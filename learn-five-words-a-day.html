How to learn new words every day without quitting in week two

Most vocabulary plans fail because they are too big: build a deck first, then face a review queue that only grows. A habit that lasts is small enough to keep on a bad day. This guide shows a simple way to learn words daily and build vocabulary in any of 44 languages, and how Wordmate Lite turns it into a one-minute, five-word trail that always ends by showing the exact daily words you just learned.

Why five words a day works

  • It fits any day. One quiet minute, one subway stop, one coffee.
  • Nothing to set up. No decks to build before you start.
  • You see the result. The closing screen shows the five words you just learned.
  • The streak survives bad days. A small goal is a goal you can still hit when you are tired.

A daily vocabulary routine with Wordmate Lite

  1. Pick a language and a life path. 44 learning languages and 14 paths, including travel, business, exams, daily life, dining, shopping, interviews, health, tech, nature, entertainment and social talk.
  2. Choose your level. Each path is broken into small units at beginner, intermediate and advanced level.
  3. Walk the five-word trail. Tap any word to hear it read aloud by the system voice; pick the voice you prefer for each language.
  4. Save the words worth keeping. Saved words are grouped by topic and level so you can come back anytime.
  5. Set a daily goal. 5, 10, 15 or 20 words, and watch your streak, longest run, today's words and progress rings rise.

Privacy and purchase

Everything runs offline. No account, no sign in, no personal data collected, no third-party advertising, no tracking. Frequently asked questions

How many languages can I learn?

There are 44 learning languages and 14 life paths.

Does Wordmate Lite work offline?

Yes. Everything runs offline, on a plane, on a subway platform or anywhere with no signal.

Can I hear the pronunciation?

Yes. Tap any word to hear it read aloud by the system voice, and choose the voice for each language.

Can I learn more than five words a day?

Yes. Set a daily goal of 5, 10, 15 or 20 words.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no account or sign in, and no personal data is collected.

Is it a subscription?

No. One language and one beginner path stay open without paying; a one-time in-app purchase unlocks everything else.

Wordmate Lite is an independent app and is not affiliated with Apple. Pronunciation uses the system voices installed on your device.
