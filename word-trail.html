What is a word trail in Wordmate Lite?

Each session is a short trail of five words. You walk it in about a minute, hear each word read aloud by the system voice, and when you finish the app shows exactly which five words you learned.

Which languages can I walk a word trail in?

44 learning languages, including Japanese, Korean and Spanish. Trails start with real situations like travel, dining and shopping.

What topics do the trails cover?

14 life paths: travel, business, exams, daily life, dining, shopping, interviews, health, technology, nature, entertainment and social talk among them. Each path is divided into small units at beginner, intermediate and advanced level.

How do I set a daily goal and see my progress?

You set a daily goal of 5, 10, 12 or 20 words. The app shows how many days in a row you have kept going, your best run, today's words, progress in each language and a small ring filling on every topic and unit.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Everything works offline, with no account and no sign-in, so it keeps going on a long flight, on the subway, or anywhere the signal drops out. No personal data is collected, there is no third-party advertising or tracking, and what you learn stays on your device.

Is it free?

Wordmate Lite is a free download. Without paying you can pick one language and one beginner path, learn your first 10 words and save up to 3; other languages, paths and levels stay visible as previews. A single one-time purchase unlocks all 44 languages, all 14 paths, every level, unlimited learning and unlimited saved words. It is not a subscription. Prices differ by country, so check the App Store page.
