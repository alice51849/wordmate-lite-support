What is Wordmate Lite?

Wordmate Lite is a vocabulary app where you learn 5 words in about a minute, in Japanese, Korean, Spanish or any of 44 languages. You walk a short five-word trail, hear each word read aloud by the system voice, and see exactly which five words you learned when you finish. There are 14 life paths, including travel, business, exams, daily life, dining, shopping, interviews, health, tech, nature, entertainment and social talk, each split into small units at beginner, intermediate and advanced level. It tracks your streak and progress and works offline.

Who is Wordmate Lite for?

Adults who have started a language three times and stopped three times, commuters with a few minutes between subway stops, travelers packing for next week, students preparing for exams, professionals who need the vocabulary of their own field, and anyone who wants a small daily habit. You can set a daily goal of 5, 10, 12 or 20 words.

How does Wordmate Lite charge?

Wordmate Lite is a free download. Without paying you can pick one language and one beginner path, learn your first 10 words and save up to 3. Other languages, paths and levels stay visible as previews. A single one-time purchase unlocks all 44 languages, all 14 paths, every level, unlimited learning and unlimited saved words. It is not a subscription. Prices differ by country, so check the App Store page. An Android version is listed on Google Play.

How is Wordmate Lite different from other ways to learn vocabulary?

Based on what the listing describes: each session is a five-word trail that ends by showing the five words you learned; you can choose the voice you prefer for each language; saved words are grouped by topic and level; progress shows days in a row, your best run, today's words and a ring for each topic and unit. Everything runs offline, on a plane or a subway platform. There is no account or sign-in, no personal data is collected, and there is no third-party advertising or tracking.
